=== 1. Fidelity Social Sentiment S-Score (model by Social Market Analytics / SMA) ===
URL: https://www.fidelity.com/webcontent/ap110398-researchsnapshot-content/16.13/help/Learn_More_About_Social_Sentiment.pdf — Status: retrieved (full 2pp PDF).
SCALE: "S-Scores range from -4.25 to 4.25. Scores between 0 and 1 or 0 and -1 are considered to be neutral." Defined as "a normalized representation of social media sentiment over a lookback period ... measures the deviation of changes in sentiment intensity of a given stock." Interpretation: "High positive sentiment indicates a security is statistically likely to move higher. High negative sentiment indicates the security is statistically likely to go lower."
PER-MESSAGE SCORING / NORMALIZATION: NOT in the Fidelity PDF (it only says messages are "bucket[ed] and weigh[ed] ... group[ed] into time periods" then "normalization and scoring"; Fidelity defers to a separate SMA "methodology document"). Vendor successor Context Analytics states: messages "fine-grain scored from -1.0000 to 1.0000", then "exponentially time-weight summed by security over a 24-hour rolling period. A 20-day mean and standard deviation are taken as historical baselines to create a standardized score" (+ statistical-significance flag) ⇒ S-Score ≈ (24h EW sentiment − 20-day mean)/20-day std dev. [Formula UNVERIFIED against the Fidelity source itself; only from vendor FAQ.]
WEIGHTING (recency half-life, source quality, duplication, volume): UNVERIFIED — no numeric weights, half-life, dedupe or volume scaling published in either source. Only qualitative "Evaluate" step: filter for financial relevance, remove spam, "determine whether the message is from sources SMA believes to be relevant."
BUCKETS: only neutral band (0 to ±1) published; no other cut-points. UNVERIFIED.
AGGREGATION/WINDOW/CADENCE: pipeline Extract→Evaluate→Calculate; "SMA monitors the Twitter and Stock Twits message streams for messages about 4200 different publicly-traded companies on a minute-by-minute basis." Vendor: 24h rolling window; 1-min/15-min/daily signals (custom). Sources: Twitter + StockTwits ("separate, independently selectable ... not a single bundled feed"). Coverage: 4,200 companies (Fidelity); vendor: US equities 5,500+, ETFs 3,500+, futures 100+, FX 50 pairs, crypto 850+, private 700+, intl TSX/NSE/LSE/ASX/JPX/Pan-EU.
VALIDATION: none in Fidelity ("Fidelity has not validated the integrity of this data"). Vendor backtest chart "Price > $5 Universe: 2011-12-01 to 7/22/26" + qualitative alpha/drawdown claims; NO IC/hit-rate/Sharpe/alpha numbers. UNVERIFIED numerically.
CAVEATS (Fidelity): rapid movement makes it time-sensitive; posting volume may be limited; anonymous/unverifiable posts; market-manipulation/impersonation risk; volume unpredictable. Fidelity: "Fidelity has not validated the integrity of this data."

=== 2. AlphaSense Sentiment Score ===
URL: https://www.alpha-sense.com/blog/engineering/sentiment-score/ — Status: retrieved (full article, 2024-10-03).
SCALE: "positive 100 to negative 100, with zero being neutral" (normalised display −99…99).
FORMULAE: "Each sentence in a transcript gets a prediction of positive, negative or neutral along with a confidence value (i.e. 99% confidence...)"; doc score = (#positive statements − #negative statements)/total statements ("Raw sentiment is the percent of the document that is positive minus the percent that is negative"). QoQ "delta (Δ) ... difference in total sentiment from the previous quarter's earnings transcript."
NORMALIZATION/WEIGHTING: "normalized across all companies such that the mean sentiment is 0 and the scores are stretched between -99 and 99," by (a) "the confidence of each prediction" and (b) "the mean and standard deviation of scores across all transcripts in the last two years." Percentiles: 0=average; ±40=top/bottom 20%; ±99=top/bottom 2%. Cross-sectional, not per-company z-score.
AGGREGATION: unit = document/earnings transcript (no per-ticker daily/weekly aggregation described); comparison by cross-company ranking and QoQ delta. Model: ML/NLP, "trained on 10+ years of human-curated financial documents"; scores "each sentence by word or phrase."
VALIDATION: "our sentiment analysis model has been shown to be over 90% accurate" — no sample, period, label definition, benchmark or CI. UNVERIFIED specifics. Limitations: sarcasm, word ambiguity, multipolarity.

=== 3. MarketGrader Sentiment Score ===
URL: https://www.marketgrader.com/blog/understanding-the-marketgrader-sentiment-score/ — Status: retrieved (full article, 2022-11-04).
INPUTS (4 indicators, each graded nine-point A+…F daily):
 (1) Price Momentum — MACD, daily price change vs 12-day and 26-day MAs; graded from MACD line vs zero, MACD line vs MACD Average, Oscillator direction vs zero.
 (2) Price Trend — 50/100/200-day price returns ranked absolute + relative to broad market and sector, plus risk-adjusted (beta vs market & sector) returns over same periods.
 (3) Relative Strength — RSI-based over 50/100/200 days, "giving a greater weight to the short-term periods"; based on change in rankings across the three periods; uses returns and risk-adjusted returns vs market and peer group.
 (4) Earnings Guidance — 3-month change in consensus EPS estimates (current vs 1/2/3 months ago); next fiscal year unless FY-end within 3 months, then following year.
SCORE/WEIGHTING: each letter → numeric; combined into 0–10. "A+ in all four ... maximum Sentiment Score of 10, ... F in all four ... minimum ... zero." "The four sentiment indicators are not equally weighted" — individual weights NOT disclosed (UNVERIFIED). Missing-indicator rule: without analyst consensus, Earnings Guidance dropped and its weight "allocated proportionally to the relative weights of the three indicators."
BANDS (explicit): ≤3.9 'Negative'; 4–6.9 'Neutral'; ≥7 'Positive'. CADENCE: "real-time indicator ... calculated daily." Sits alongside GARP: 24 fundamental indicators drive overall grade + Buy/Hold/Sell; Sentiment is a separate near-term signal.
VALIDATION: qualitative only — higher near-term price correlation than fundamentals; "predictive value within a forward-looking rolling window of 30 to 60 days." No sample/IC/hit-rate. UNVERIFIED numerically.

=== ENGINE-RELEVANT CONTRAST (engine inputs: OHLCV, quarterly/annual fundamentals, options chain w/ IV, news headlines + relevance 0-100, analyst ratings + price targets, prediction-market probs, FRED macro) ===
• MarketGrader is the only locally computable template: MACD(12,26), 50/100/200-day absolute+relative returns, market/sector beta, RSI, 3-month consensus-EPS revisions. Gaps: weights undisclosed (only letter→numeric + bands reproducible); needs a market benchmark and sector/peer grouping; engine has ratings + price targets but NO consensus-EPS estimate history needed by Earnings Guidance (proxy only, not MarketGrader's method).
• AlphaSense not directly computable (corpus = earnings transcripts, absent). Reusable: sentence labels {pos,neg,neu}+confidence; doc = (#pos−#neg)/#total, confidence-weighted; cross-sectional z/percentile vs trailing 2-year universe (0=avg, ±40=top/bottom 20%, ±99=top/bottom 2%). Relevance/100 can proxy confidence; QoQ delta → aggregate-headline QoQ change.
• Fidelity/SMA not computable (needs Twitter/StockTwits firehose, spam filter, source-authority graph; engine has no social volume). Adapter pattern: signed headline sentiment in [−1,+1] × relevance/100, EW into 24h aggregate, z-score vs trailing 20-day mean/std, clip ±4.25, neutral ±1.
• All: only AlphaSense publishes an explicit document formula and bands; Fidelity/SMA and MarketGrader are proprietary-weight black boxes; AlphaSense's sole performance claim is a bare ">90% accurate." Treat all as directional templates; calibrate weights on own history rather than claiming exact replication.
