### Financial Sentiment Analysis Using FinBERT with Application in Predicting Stock Movement (Zeng & Jiang, arXiv:2306.02136v3, 11 Jun 2025) - https://arxiv.org/html/2306.02136v3
- **Status**: retrieved (full text; MathML formulas rendered).
- **Formulae**: Checkpoint `ProsusAI/finbert` (HF); baseline `bert-base-uncased`. FinBERT provenance = BERT further trained on financial corpus (Reuters TRC2 + Financial PhraseBank, Malo et al. 2014), per Araci 2019 (arXiv:1908.10063). Off-the-shelf, NO fine-tuning here. Labels {positive,negative,neutral}; softmax P(class_i)=e^{logit_i}/SUM_j e^{logit_j}, i in {positive,negative,neutral}. Per-article scalar SentimentScore = P(positive) - P(negative), continuous, 'ranging from -1 to 1'. PER-DAY AGGREGATION (exact): `SentimentScore_t = (1/N_t) * SUM_{n=1}^{N_t} [ P_n(positive) - P_n(negative) ]`, where N_t = number of news articles for a given stock ticker on day t; P_n(positive), P_n(negative) = FinBERT sentiment probabilities for the n-th news article. NSI: NSI_t = 1 if return_t > s; 0 if -s <= return_t <= s; -1 if return_t < -s; return_t = (ClosePrice_t - OpenPrice_t)/OpenPrice_t; **s = 0.01**. LSTM input X_t = [S_t, O_{t-1},H_{t-1},L_{t-1},V_{t-1}, ..., C_{t-k},O_{t-k},H_{t-k},L_{t-k},V_{t-k}], k=60 look-back days. Min-Max x_scaled=(x-x_min)/(x_max-x_min) to [0,1], scalers fit on training data (inputs O,H,L,V; separate scaler for Close target).
- **Aggregation / window**: point-in-time daily; each article bucketed by publication day per ticker; non-trading days (weekends/US holidays) -> sentiment aligned to NEXT available trading day. Unweighted arithmetic mean over articles (N_t).
- **Signal**: NO thresholded trading signal. The daily sentiment score is an LSTM feature; prediction target = next trading day's close price (regression). The ONLY threshold in the paper is s=0.01 for NSI labelling. Training: MSE loss, Adam lr 0.001, <=100 epochs, batch size 1, early stopping patience 20 (val MSE). Architecture: LSTM(100, return_sequences=True) -> LSTM(100, return_sequences=False) -> Dense(25, ReLU) -> Dense(1, linear); tanh recurrent / sigmoid gates.
- **Split**: strict temporal 90% train / 10% test (first 90% of days train). Validation = 10% of the training data taken chronologically from the end. 80/20 tried first, worse. Tokenisation: FinBERT tokenizer, padding + truncation, max sequence length 512; input = news TITLE only.
- **Dataset**: merge of Kaggle 'Historical Financial News Archive' (>800 US companies, 12 years to 2019; date, ticker, title, body) + 'Daily Financial News for 6000+ Stocks' (analyst_ratings_processed.csv after dropping irrelevant cols; news after dropping category/provider/URL) -> concat + dedup on unique news titles = **1,056,471 records** (titles, dates, tickers). Prices via yfinance. Per-stock modelling; MSFT 7875 raw rows -> 1440 after preprocess.
- **Metrics (MSE train/val)**: ARIMA val 0.00975 (also MAE 0.07154, RMSE 0.09876); LSTM 1.0206e-03/1.1937e-03; BERT+LSTM 1.0216e-04/3.3132e-04; FinBERT+DNN 1.4674e-04/3.4177e-04; **FinBERT+LSTM (proposed) 9.1701e-05/3.1975e-04**. No accuracy/F1 (regression). Sentiment-free baseline = LSTM on numerical data alone (val ~3.7x worse).
- **Limitations (stated)**: small per-stock data -> overfitting (loss drops in <=3 epochs); titles only (not bodies) -> limited info; FinBERT vs base BERT gain modest (domain adaptation under-exploited, cites Peng et al. 2021); under-predicts close price long-run; no causal claim (news<->market); news ends 2019; per-stock training, no cross-sectional pooling.
- **Engine-relevant notes**: needs per-article p_pos,p_neg (softmax); title-only; 512-token truncation; daily bucket per ticker + next-trading-day alignment; dedup by unique title; unweighted mean. Engine inputs (options IV, analyst targets, prediction markets, FRED) are NOT used here.

### FinBERT Sentiment Analysis (emergentmind topic) - https://www.emergentmind.com/topics/sentiment-analysis-using-finbert
- **Status**: retrieved (inline-cited secondary synthesis; primary refs in brackets).
- **Formulae**: logits l_neg,l_neu,l_pos; p_hat_j = e^{l_j}/SUM_k e^{l_k}, j in {neg,neu,pos}; discrete label = argmax_j p_hat_j [2412.06837]; continuous score s = p_hat_pos - p_hat_neg in [-1,1]. Training loss categorical cross-entropy L_CE = -SUM_i y_i log y_hat_i.
- **Aggregation schemes documented**: (1) average s across documents AND across time intervals, 'e.g., daily aggregates'; (2) weighted merging by news topic or category [2407.16150; 2505.01432]; (3) argmax modal/discrete class. Neutral: a full class - under argmax neutral wins when maximal; under s=p_pos-p_neg neutral is the implicit residual (p_neu = 1 - p_pos - p_neg).
- **Normalisation / weighting**: softmax; 'possibility of weighted merging by news topic or category'. Hyperparams: batch 8-32, epochs 2-10, AdamW, lr 1e-6 to 5e-5, early stopping, AMP; max seq len commonly 128 (512 for finbert-lc).
- **Calibration**: 'No explicit probability calibration (such as Platt scaling or isotonic regression) is generally applied in baseline implementations, though such methods are proposed for future improvements' [2412.06837]. Best practice: 'Deploy calibration where probabilistic outputs are used for trading or risk management.'
- **Imbalance**: 'residual class imbalance sensitivity remain active areas'; label regimes binary / 3-class / aspect-based; labels expert-annotated / event-mapped / pseudo-labelled.
- **Validation evidence**: Financial PhraseBank fine-tuned FinBERT accuracy 0.88, F1 0.87 (> GPT-4o zero/few-shot <=0.86/0.85; > GPT-3.5, RoBERTa); macro-F1 +3-6pp over general transformers; zero-shot Bayesian-fusion PhraseBank acc 0.9690 / macro-F1 0.9593 (FinBERT direct influence 0.364 on decision node). FinBERT-LSTM NASDAQ-100 accuracy 92.8% (LSTM-only) -> 95.5%. African equities: FinBERT 63% acc / 65% ROC AUC vs tuned logistic regression 82% / 90%; FinBERT ~100 min GPU vs seconds. TinyFinBERT 99% of teacher accuracy at 1/7.5 size; fine-tuning up to 40GB GPU.
- **Comparison between schemes**: NOT quantified - no head-to-head mean vs vote vs weighted numbers. Decayed / rolling-window aggregation and explicit threshold selection are NOT documented in this source.
- **Engine-relevant notes**: directly reusable = mean of s; weighted variant = relevance weighting. Warns probabilities are uncalibrated (bad for threshold trading) and class-imbalanced (PhraseBank priors).

### Financial News Sentiment Analysis using FinBERT (Raviraj Shinde, Medium, 2021-10-10) - https://medium.com/@ravirajshinde2000/financial-news-sentiment-analysis-using-finbert-25afcc95e65f ; code: github.com/Raviraj2000/Financial-News-Sentiment-Analysis-using-FinBERT
- **Status**: retrieved (article text + primary notebook `SentimentAnalysis.ipynb` recovered from GitHub; article's inline code was images).
- **Implementation**: model = BertForSequenceClassification.from_pretrained('yiyanghkust/finbert-tone', num_labels=3); tokenizer = BertTokenizer.from_pretrained('yiyanghkust/finbert-tone').
- **Tokenisation**: `tokenizer(x, return_tensors='pt', padding=True)` - NO truncation and NO explicit max_length / sequence-length cap (default BERT special tokens). Headlines tokenised individually; **batch = 1, no DataLoader/batching**.
- **Label mapping**: `labels = {0:'neutral', 1:'positive', 2:'negative'}`; assigned by `np.argmax(outputs)` -> MODAL class (NOT probability difference). Matches finbert-tone model card (LABEL_0 neutral, LABEL_1 positive, LABEL_2 negative). ProsusAI/finbert numeric label order = UNVERIFIED (card states only 'positive, negative or neutral').
- **Daily aggregation**: NONE. Reads news.csv (ISO-8859-1; columns `headline`, `sentiment`), classifies each headline independently, scores with sklearn `accuracy_score(y, sent_val)`. No timestamps, no per-day score, no dedup.
- **Worked numbers**: accuracy ~= 80%.
- **Limitations**: headline-only; argmax discards probabilities; per-headline loop; no temporal aggregation.
- **Engine-relevant notes**: supplies a concrete tokenisation/label-mapping template (finbert-tone, argmax) but NO daily-score methodology; if the engine adopts finbert-tone, note its class order differs from ProsusAI's.

### Cross-source engine calibration notes (0-100 relevance -> mean-of-probabilities)
- The published formula consumes ARTICLE-LEVEL class probabilities (p_pos,p_neg), not a relevance score. A 0-100 relevance score is not (p_pos,p_neg); there is NO documented transform relevance -> probabilities. Using 0-100 directly as sentiment is INVALID unless that score is itself produced by a calibrated sentiment classifier with its own softmax.
- Valid path A (faithful): run FinBERT on each headline -> softmax -> (p_pos,p_neg) -> S_t = (1/N_t)SUM(p_pos-p_neg). Relevance unused.
- Valid path B (weighted generalization, matches emergentmind 'weighted merging'): S_t = SUM_n w_n(p_pos,n - p_neg,n) / SUM_n w_n, w_n = relevance_n/100. This is a DEFINED generalization, NOT the published unweighted formula.
- What invalidates the mean: (i) treating an unsigned relevance score as signed sentiment (loses direction, forces S_t>=0); (ii) relevance correlating with article length/importance/N_t, biasing S_t toward high-relevance days; (iii) neutral mass dropped - all-neutral relevant news gives S_t~0 indistinguishable from no news; (iv) uncalibrated probabilities (emergentmind) making thresholded trades unjustified.
- Timestamps: bucket by ticker-local trading day; paper aligns non-trading-day news to the NEXT trading day, so after-close/weekend articles enter the next session's S_t; intraday vs overnight publication changes the day fed. Dedup by unique headline to stop syndicated duplicates inflating N_t.
- Engine inputs the sources DO NOT use: per-article (p_pos,p_neg) probabilities (only relevance 0-100 available), options/IV, analyst ratings/targets, prediction-market probabilities, FRED macro series. None of the three sources builds a thresholded trade signal.
